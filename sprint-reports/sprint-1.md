# Sprint 1 Report
## 1. Problem Analysis Our project focuses on space equipment left behind on the Moon, Mars, and in different orbits after completing their missions.
## 2. Project Objectives 
- Understand why spacecraft are left behind after their missions.
- Explore the effects of abandoned space equipment.
- Find possible solutions to reduce space debris.
- Raise awareness about protecting space.
## 3. Research and Findings We researched different spacecraft and missions, including Dawn, NEAR Shoemaker, Pioneer 10 and 11, Ingenuity, and Kepler. We explored their missions and what happened to them after their missions ended. 
## 4. Next Steps We will continue researching abandoned space equipment and develop ideas for reducing space debris
